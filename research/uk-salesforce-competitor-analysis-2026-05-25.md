## SIMPALA EXTRA-DEPTH ANALYSIS

### Why Simpala Ranks #1 Despite Being Newer and a Lower Tier Partner

**Exact-match page title:** "Salesforce **Consultants** & Summit Partner **UK**" — contains the two most searched terms in the UK market. Even though Simpala may be Ridge not Summit, the title claim drives organic CTR.

**Topic clustering — Field Service Lightning:**
Simpala has built the deepest content cluster in the UK for Salesforce Field Service:
- Dedicated capability page (/capability/field-service/) — 1,800–2,000 words, H1 "Salesforce Field Service Consultants"
- 8+ blog posts about Field Service
- Field Service Quick Start package (£13,300, transparent pricing)
- 3+ Field Service case studies
- Field Service ROI Calculator (listed in nav under Resources)
- Event coverage (Playing the Field Service LIVE)
- eBook download on Field Service

This is textbook **topical authority** — Google rewards sites that comprehensively cover a specific topic.

**Publishing consistency:** Simpala has published at least one post per month since mid-2024. Their most recent post was April 2026 (reflective Field Service implementation essay by a named author: Sarah Machin, Business Change Consultant).

**Named author strategy:** Recent Simpala post bylined to "Sarah Machin, Business Change Consultant" — a signal of E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust) which Google's guidelines increasingly reward.

**Internal linking:** Their "What is [Product]?" blog posts link back to capability pages, which link to case studies, which link to Quick Start packages. A coherent content funnel.

**Unique product offering (BookNow):** Integration with leisure/entertainment booking platforms — a niche vertical that likely faces less competition and generates referral traffic.

**Transparent pricing removes sales barrier:** The £13,300 Quick Start and £500 Health Check pricing removes one of the biggest B2B conversion barriers. Prospects self-qualify and book directly.

**Missing from Simpala (their weaknesses):**
- No custom meta descriptions on most pages
- No FAQ schema markup
- Homepage lacks hard numbers (no "X projects delivered" stat)
- Founders page doesn't list specific Salesforce certifications
- No ISO or Cyber Essentials certifications
- Relatively thin case studies (no hard ROI metrics on most)
- No dedicated location page for London despite ranking for London terms

## PRIORITY RECOMMENDATIONS (Ordered by Impact)

### Quick Wins (1–2 weeks)

1. **Change homepage meta title** to include "Salesforce Consultants"
   From: "Appdraft - Salesforce Implementation Experts | London UK"
   To: "Appdraft: Salesforce Consultants & Ridge Partner | London UK"

2. **Update H1s on all cloud/product pages** to "[Product] Consultants" format
   Example: "Salesforce Sales Cloud Consultants" not "Salesforce Sales Cloud"

3. **Add partnership tier badge** above the fold on homepage
   "Salesforce Ridge Consulting Partner" displayed prominently with badge graphic

### Content Priorities (1–3 months)

4. **Resume blog publishing immediately** — minimum 1 post/month
   Start with "What is Salesforce [Product]?" posts for Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Field Service, Marketing Cloud, Agentforce. These are 1,200–1,500 words each and should target the same informational keywords Simpala is winning.

5. **Publish 5–10 case studies** with named clients (or anonymised by sector)
   Format: Problem → Solution → Results (with at least one metric). Ideally: client name, industry, Salesforce products used.

6. **Expand product/cloud pages** to 1,500–2,000 words
   Each page should have: H1 "[Product] Consultants", H2 sections covering "What is [Product]?", "Key Features", "Why [Product]?", "Our [Product] Implementation Process", customer quote, CTA. Add internal links to relevant blog posts and case studies.

7. **Create a Salesforce Health Check landing page with defined pricing and deliverables**
   Model: Simpala's £500 / 1 week offer. Appdraft already has /services/health-checks — add transparent pricing and a deliverables list.

### Medium-Term (3–6 months)

8. **Build a team/consultants page** with individual profiles
   Named consultants, Salesforce certification count, years of experience. This supports E-E-A-T and builds trust.

9. **Create downloadable lead magnet**
   "Salesforce Implementation Checklist" or "CRM Mistakes Guide" — gated with email capture. Model: Silver Softworks' SaaS Playbook.

10. **Pick a specialist niche and build a content cluster**
    Recommend: Field Service (Simpala already owns this), OR Agentforce (emerging, less competition), OR Financial Services (Silver Softworks owns it but Appdraft has sector experience)
    Build: 6–8 blog posts + 1 capability page + 2–3 case studies + 1 guide = topical authority

11. **Consider ISO 9001 / Cyber Essentials certification**
    Silver Softworks uses these heavily with enterprise/regulated sector clients. Cyber Essentials is relatively cheap and quick. Strong trust signal for procurement processes.
